## Design Context

### Users

The first release is a personal golf tracker that is account-ready for additional golfers. It is used on a phone outdoors during rounds for quick scoring, at a range or simulator for session capture, and later on larger screens for reviewing progress.

### Brand Personality

Focused, capable, and motivating. The interface should feel like a quiet performance room: serious about improvement without becoming clinical or aggressive.

### Aesthetic Direction

Retain the dark, high-contrast golf performance aesthetic with turf greens and a sharp lime accent. Prioritize legibility in daylight, clear numeric hierarchy, and compact editorial details. Avoid generic dashboard cards, decorative gradients, and tiny desktop controls carried onto mobile.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next scoring action reachable with one thumb.
2. Keep every primary touch target at least 44 by 44 pixels.
3. Preserve all core functions on mobile and progressively reveal secondary detail.
4. Show save, loading, error, and authentication state clearly.
5. Keep account ownership and data privacy visible but unobtrusive.
